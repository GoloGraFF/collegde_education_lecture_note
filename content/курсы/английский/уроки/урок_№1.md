## 2. Читай после прослушивания: The Last Train

Anna had missed the last train. She stood on the empty platform and stared at the timetable, as if the numbers might change. It was nearly midnight, and a cold wind was blowing from the river.

"Great," she muttered. "Just great."

She had stayed at the office too long again. Her boss had asked her to finish a report, and she hadn't been able to say no. Now her phone was almost dead, and the station café had closed an hour earlier.

Then she noticed an old man sitting on a bench at the far end of the platform. He was wearing a long grey coat and holding a paper bag on his knees. He looked calm, as if he were waiting for something that would definitely come.

Anna hesitated, then walked over. "Excuse me," she said. "Do you know if there are any more trains tonight?"

The man looked up and smiled. "Not tonight," he said. "But there's a night bus at half past twelve. It stops right outside."

"Thank you," Anna said, relieved. She sat down next to him. For a while neither of them spoke.

"Would you like one?" he asked suddenly, and opened the bag. It was full of warm chestnuts.

Anna laughed. "Where did you get them at this hour?"

"I roast them myself," he said. "Every night I bring a bag here. Someone always misses the last train."

She took one. It was hot and sweet, and for the first time that evening she didn't feel tired.

When the bus finally arrived, the old man didn't move.

"Aren't you coming?" Anna asked.

"Oh, no," he replied, and winked. "I live just around the corner. I only come for the company."

## 4. Грамматика: Past Perfect

**had + третья форма глагола**: действие случилось раньше другого момента в прошлом.

- *Anna **had missed** the last train.* Поезд ушёл до того, как она пришла на платформу.
- *Her boss **had asked** her to finish a report.* Просьба была раньше, чем история на вокзале.
- *The station café **had closed** an hour earlier.*

**Ошибки:**

- Пропуск was: *she late* → *she **was** late*.
- will + -ing: *she will waiting* → *she **will wait*** (или *she will be waiting*).

**Не понял:** chestnuts, was blowing, noticed, half past twelve.
