## 3. Читай после прослушивания: The Wrong Umbrella

It was raining hard when Tom left the bookshop. He was tired and hungry, and he was thinking about dinner, so he grabbed the first black umbrella he saw by the door and hurried out into the street.

He had walked two blocks before he noticed the name on the handle. It said "Clark" in small gold letters. It wasn't his umbrella at all.

"Oh no," he muttered, and turned back.

When he reached the shop, the door was locked. The lights were still on, though, and a young woman was standing inside. She was looking at the empty umbrella stand with a worried face.

Tom knocked on the glass. She hesitated, then opened the door a little.

"I think this is yours," he said, and held out the umbrella. "I'm so sorry. I took it by mistake."

The woman stared at it, then laughed. "I thought someone had stolen it! It was my grandfather's. I was going to walk home without it."

"Where do you live?" Tom asked.

"Just around the corner. But it's still pouring."

Tom looked at the rain, then at the umbrella. "Then we'll share it," he said. "I'll walk you home."

She smiled, relieved. "I'm Mary," she said. "Mary Clark."

They walked slowly under the old umbrella. Neither of them was in a hurry any more. By the time they reached her door, the rain had stopped, but Tom didn't notice. He was listening to her stories about her grandfather.

"Will you come back to the shop tomorrow?" Mary asked.

"I will," Tom said. "I'll need my own umbrella." And he winked.

*Знакомые слова из прошлого урока: muttered, hesitated, stared, relieved, around the corner, noticed, winked.*

## 6. Грамматика: was/were + -ing

Past Continuous описывает процесс в прошлом: **was/were + глагол-ing**. Без was/were так нельзя.

- ✅ *He **was thinking** about dinner.* ❌ *He thinking about dinner.*
- ✅ *A young woman **was standing** inside.*

Часто идёт вместе с Past Simple: длинное действие (фон) и короткое событие: *It **was raining** hard when Tom **left** the bookshop.*

Про будущее: после **will** глагол идёт без -ing. *I'**ll walk** you home.* ❌ *I will walking.*

Задание: найди в тексте ещё 2 примера с was/were + -ing.

> [!check]- Ответ

**Ошибки:**

- Снова пропуск be: *she going to walk* → *she was going to walk*, *they are go* → *they went*. Тема добавлена в [[План курса#Частые ошибки и трудные темы|частые ошибки]].
- *I don't want give it up* → *I don't want **to** give it up.*
- *he grab* → *he **grabbed***; *althought* → *although*.

**Что не расслышал в диктанте:**

- *someone **had** stolen it*: had в быстрой речи сжимается до «əd» и сливается с someone.
- *we'll* (wiːl, долгое «ии») ≠ *will* (wɪl, короткое «и»).
- *noticed the* → *known this*: начало noticed звучит как know, безударное the легко принять за this.

**Не вспомнил:** missed, half past twelve, exhausted, discouraged.
